The Recovery Lottery

Three small steps you can take today

Big change starts like compound interest: small amounts, added consistently. Each step below has a link to get you started.

  1. 01

    Vote. Register every election year.

    A diaspora that votes forces politicians to answer to the people funding the country.

    Register to vote from abroadCentral Election Commission of BiH · e-Registracija → How registration abroad worksizbori.ba · free, done online →
  2. 02

    Teach your kids Bosnian.

    The next generation can only invest in, return to, or fight for a country they can still talk to.

    Bosnian for kids and teensBosnian2GO · online classes →
  3. 03

    Get investment ready.

    Settle your family's papers: finish inheritance cases, register property in your name, and keep your Bosnian documents valid. Then move your money through a bank, not a bus. No fund can invest money it can't see.

    Diaspora rights guideMinistry for Human Rights and Refugees · documents, citizenship, property (PDF, in Bosnian) → Check your family's propertyKatastar.ba · Federation of BiH cadastre lookup →

    In Republika Srpska, use e-Katastar (RGURS).
